# 🚀 KLYFT — Intelligent DCA Management Platform  
**Reimagining how enterprises manage overdue recoveries through AI, automation, and transparency.**

---

## 🌟 Overview

Klyft is a modern, AI-powered **Debt Collection Agency (DCA) Management Platform** designed to transform how enterprises manage overdue recoveries.  
It replaces fragmented, manual workflows—such as spreadsheets, emails, and ad-hoc follow-ups—with a **centralized, automated, and insight-driven system** that improves recovery speed, accountability, and governance.

Built for the **FedEx Smart Hackathon**, Klyft demonstrates how digital platforms and AI can make recovery operations **faster, smarter, and more predictable**.

---

## 🎯 Problem We’re Solving

Today, enterprises face major challenges in managing DCAs:

- Case data scattered across emails and Excel files  
- Delayed and inconsistent updates from DCAs  
- No real-time visibility into recovery progress  
- Weak audit trails and growing compliance risks  
- Little to no predictability of recovery outcomes  

👉 The result: **slow recoveries, poor accountability, and missed opportunities.**

---

## 💡 Our Solution

Recora introduces a **centralized, AI-driven recovery ecosystem** that:

- Automates case creation and assignment  
- Enables real-time collaboration with DCAs  
- Enforces SOPs and SLAs through workflows  
- Predicts recovery outcomes using AI  
- Provides leadership with actionable insights  

---

## 🧩 Key Features

### ⚙️ Smart Automation  
Automatic case creation when invoices become overdue and intelligent DCA assignment based on performance and capacity.

### 🤖 AI-Driven Intelligence  
Predicts recovery probability, prioritizes high-impact cases, and recommends next best actions.

### 🔄 Workflow & SLA Automation  
Automated reminders, escalations, and reassignment eliminate manual follow-ups.

### 🤝 Structured Collaboration  
Secure, role-based portals for DCAs to update cases and upload documents.

### 📊 Real-Time Insights  
Dashboards for ageing trends, recovery performance, and DCA effectiveness.

---

## 🗺️ How It Works

1. An invoice becomes overdue and is automatically converted into a recovery case.  
2. The system assigns the case to the best-performing DCA.  
3. DCAs take action and update progress in real time.  
4. Workflows monitor SLAs and trigger escalations when needed.  
5. AI and dashboards provide visibility into outcomes and future recovery trends.
